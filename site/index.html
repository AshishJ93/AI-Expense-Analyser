05 — Briefing

A paragraph written from the figures.

After the math, a short briefing can be written from those totals. If a number in the paragraph and a card disagree, the card wins. The full statement is not sent.

Briefing

Your briefing

Rent is the largest category in the demo, at ₹1,92,000. October spending rose 96.9% from September, and shopping moved from ₹2,250 to ₹74,210. Weekend food delivery averaged ₹1,119 a day. Seven subscriptions recur at about ₹3,069 a month.

06 — Keep

A report, and the rows behind it.

Categories and merchants explain the total. Transactions let you search and correct a category. Reports export a PDF or Excel of the view you are looking at. Nothing is written to disk on the server.
